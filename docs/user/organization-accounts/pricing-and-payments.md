# Organization Pricing and Payment Terms

## Corporate Organizations

### Pricing

* $5 per member User, per month

### Payment Terms

Invoiced monthly, based on usage. Payment is due upon receipt and will be charged to the billing information on file.

### Organizations without a paid seat

Continued use of a Corporate Organization requires a paid subscription with at least
1 seat purchased. If your Organization does not have one, we will email its owners a
date to purchase a seat by, and remind them 30, 14, and 3 days before that date. If no
seat is purchased by the end of that date (UTC), we deactivate the Organization.
Purchasing a seat before then stops the emails and keeps the Organization active.
We send these emails to each owner's verified email addresses. If no owner has one
and the Organization owns no projects, it is still deactivated on the same schedule.

A deactivated Organization cannot upload new releases to its projects or invite
members, and its public page on PyPI is removed. Its projects stay on PyPI and can
still be installed. To reactivate it, purchase a seat from the Organization's billing
page within 90 days of deactivation. After that, or for help, email
[support+orgs@pypi.org](mailto:support+orgs@pypi.org).

If you no longer want the Organization, you can delete it yourself from its settings
page. Deleting an Organization requires that it owns no projects, so remove or
transfer any projects first. After deletion, you must go through the Organization
application process again to get a new one, and the name becomes available, so
someone else might register it.

## Community Organizations

### Pricing

All current Organizations features of PyPI are provided to qualifying Community Organizations
at no cost.

### Payment Terms

No payment or billing information is required.
